## Source and disclosure policy

The plugin contains no transaction records and no copied legislation. Runtime law excerpts remain derived source material. Retain the corpus's required Register attribution with any reproduced excerpt and refer to the authoritative [Federal Register](https://www.legislation.gov.au) before reliance. The current corpus licence gives the attribution date as 31 October 2026; do not automatically refresh that date without a refreshed source.

Private development push is not approval to change public visibility. No scholarly claims, claims of legal correctness, or observed task improvements follow from this build alone.
